---
title: Migration
author: Matan Kaminer
bio: Matan Kaminer is an anthropologist, an activist and a lecturer at Queen
  Mary University London. His research is concerned with labour migration,
  colonialism, ecology, theology and other aspects of life and death in
  Palestine/Israel.
summary: >
  People have been moving into Palestine/Israel for thousands of years, but
  following the Nakba and the establishment of the Israeli state the presence in
  the country of people who are neither Jewish nor Arab (NJNAs) has been
  invisibilized. This contribution begins to imagine a decolonising project that
  includes NJNAs, mobilising ethical injunctions from the Jewish and Muslim
  traditions that valorise migration and cut against (bi-)nationalism.
